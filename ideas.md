# Welfare Pride Intranet — Visual Direction

The portal should feel like a **warm, trustworthy employee home base**, not a generic enterprise admin template. The approved Welfare Pride hand-and-growth mark anchors the identity. The interface uses **Roboto** throughout, exact brand blue **`#5A92C9`** for primary actions and trust signals, and exact orange **`#D58240`** for community-oriented emphasis, section eyebrows, and selected secondary controls.

The persistent navigation creates a stable workplace frame. Content uses generous spacing, soft elevation rather than heavy borders, 16–28 px rounded corners, and readable card groupings. The dashboard hero is the main emotional brand moment: blue ground, an orange community accent, subtle grid texture, welcoming employee copy, and a strong white action. Repeated circular and rounded motifs echo the four hands around the Welfare Pride growth mark without copying the logo literally.

The experience should remain professional and calm. Avoid ornamental gradients, loud animation, glass-heavy effects, novelty illustration, or generic purple SaaS styling. Motion is limited to short transform and opacity feedback and is disabled for reduced-motion users. Empty and unconfigured states should feel intentional and helpful rather than unfinished.

Administration pages retain the same employee-hub language while communicating elevated risk through clear authorization badges, secure credential explanations, confirmation gates, and compact audit history. Microsoft and cloud-provider terminology should support the Welfare Pride brand rather than visually replace it.
